Note to all staff: please ignore the 2024 version of this policy. It has been replaced by this page.

Use the Fernway Connect VPN whenever you work from outside a Fernway office. Sign in with your work account and approve the prompt on your phone.

Don't use the VPN for personal streaming. Split tunnelling is off, so all traffic goes through Fernway while you're connected.
