Forgot your password or locked out? Reset it yourself at https://help.fernway.example/reset.

1. Open https://help.fernway.example/reset and enter your work email.
2. Approve the sign-in prompt on your phone.
3. Choose a new password of at least 14 characters.

IT will never ask for your current password. If the reset page doesn't work, contact the IT service desk at it@fernway.example.
