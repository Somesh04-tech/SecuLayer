Book flights and hotels through the travel portal at https://fernway.example/travel.

- Hotels: up to $180 per night in most cities, $240 in New York and San Francisco.
- Flights: economy class for trips under six hours.
- Meals: up to $65 per day, with receipts.

Submit travel expenses within 30 days of your return.
