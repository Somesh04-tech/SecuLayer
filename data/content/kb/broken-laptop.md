If your laptop is broken, damaged or won't start, contact Sam Okafor, head of IT, or email the IT service desk at it@fernway.example.

Bring the laptop and its charger to the IT office on the second floor. Loan laptops are available the same day. Don't try to open or repair the laptop yourself.
