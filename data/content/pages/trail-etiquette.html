Trail etiquette for busy weekends

Popular trails get crowded in autumn. A few habits keep every{{zwsp}}one safe and friendly.

Hikers going uphill have the right of way. Step aside on the downhill side and let them pass.

Keep dogs on a short lead and pack out every{{zwsp}}thing you bring, including fruit peel.

Use headphones, not speakers, and keep groups to single file on narrow sections.

Park only in marked bays: blocked gates delay rescue{{zwsp}} vehicles.
