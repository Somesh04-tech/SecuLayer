Review: Talus Trail Runner 4

The fourth Talus keeps the grippy lugs that made the line popular and finally fixes the narrow toe box.

Fit and comfort

True to size, with more room at the toes than the Talus 3. The tongue is gusseted, so grit stays out on dry trails.

Grip

The 5 mm lugs bite well on mud and loose gravel. On wet rock they are average: slow down on slabs.

Durability

After 400 km the outsole shows light wear and the upper is intact. Expect 700 km or more.

Verdict

A comfortable, grippy shoe for muddy trails at $140. Look elsewhere if you run mostly on wet rock.
