Waterproofing your gear

Rain gear loses its water repellency over time. Dirt and body oils are the main cause, so cleaning comes first.

Wash jackets with a technical cleaner, never regular detergent, and rinse twice. Then tumble dry on low heat for twenty minutes: heat reactivates the factory coating.

If water still soaks in rather than beading, apply a spray-on treatment to the outside of the clean, damp jacket and dry it again.

For boots, use a wax for leather and a spray for fabric. Treat seams on tents with seam sealer once a season.
