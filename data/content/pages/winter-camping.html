Winter camping checklist

Cold-weather camping rewards preparation. Start with a sleeping pad rated R-5 or higher: most heat is lost to the ground, not the air.

Bring a sleeping bag rated 5 °C below the coldest forecast, and keep tomorrow's water bottle inside the bag so it doesn't freeze.

Pack a stove that runs on an isobutane-propane mix, which keeps its pressure in the cold.

For more on layering, see Outdoor Journal's guide to layering for winter.
