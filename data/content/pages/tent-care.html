How to care for your tent

A good tent lasts a decade if you look after it. Most damage happens at home, not on the trail.

Dry it before you store it

Never pack a tent away wet for more than a day. Pitch it in the garden or hang it indoors until every seam is dry; mildew ruins coatings and leaves a smell that never washes out.

Store it loose

Keep the tent loosely folded in a large cotton sack in a cool, dry place, not in its tight stuff sack. Compression creases the fabric and breaks down the waterproof coating.

Clean gently

Sponge off mud with lukewarm water. Skip the washing machine and harsh soaps.

Check the poles

Wipe grit off pole sections and store them assembled loosely so the shock cord keeps its stretch.
