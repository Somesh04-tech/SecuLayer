Best ultralight tents of 2026

We spent three months on the trail with eleven tents under 1.5 kg. Here are the four we would buy again.

Best overall: Kestrel UL2

At 1.1 kg with a freestanding frame, the Kestrel UL2 pitches in under three minutes and shrugged off a night of 60 km/h gusts on the ridge. The vestibules are small for two packs.

Best on a budget: Sparrow 1

A single-wall tent for under $200. Condensation is the trade-off: wipe the walls before packing.

Best for tall hikers: Heron Long

An extra 20 cm of floor length and steep walls. It needs trekking poles to pitch.

Best for winter: Ptarmigan 2 Alpine

Heavier at 1.9 kg, but the only tent in the test that stayed quiet in snow.
